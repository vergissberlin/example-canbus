No frames? Check these debugging tips.

USB connected, no output

Briefly press RESET on the ESP, without BOOT. Close other serial monitors. Wrong port? Disconnect and select the other one.

“… waiting for frames”

Check CANH ↔ CANH, CANL ↔ CANL and common ground. GPIO 5 → CTX, GPIO 4 → CRX. Power CAN modules at 3.3 V only.

Check termination

Disconnect both USB power supplies. The WCMCU-230 modules already include bus termination; no external resistors are needed. Expect about 60 Ω between CANH and CANL.

Sender reports Bus-Off

The diagnostic firmware restarts after successful recovery. Repeated Bus-Off requires checking module power, GPIO connections and the CAN bus. DIAG lines show driver state and error counters; TX_ACK confirms successful CAN transmission with ACK, but not yet reception in the receiver log.
